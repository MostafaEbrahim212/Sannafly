# Complete Sales Presentation Script

## 1. Discovery Questions (Ask Before Starting)
1. How many products do you currently manage, and do they have options like sizes or colors?
2. Do you need full online checkout with payment gateways, or primarily a product catalog with WhatsApp inquiries?
3. Do your customers shop in Arabic, English, or both?

## 2. Opening (`/demo/presentation` — Slide 1)
- **Say (AR):** "أهلًا بيك. النهاردة هوريك منصة Sannafly — نظام متكامل بيشتغل كمتجر إلكتروني كامل أو ككتالوج عرض واحترافية، وبيدعم العربي والإنجليزي والـ White-Label بالكامل."
- **Say (EN):** "Today I will walk you through Sannafly — a bilingual White-Label Catalog & E-Commerce platform ready for immediate launch."

## 3. Storefront Walkthrough (`/`, `/catalog`, `/cart`)
- Show Arabic RTL & English LTR switching, Dark/Light mode, category/brand filters, product detail variations, wholesale pricing tiers, and simulated checkout.

## 4. Admin Dashboard & Catalog (`/admin`, `/admin/products`, `/admin/categories`)
- Show KPI cards, Smart Accordion sidebar, 5-step Product Wizard, and Inventory StockMovement ledger.

## 5. Customization, Orders & Payments (`/admin/customizer`, `/admin/orders`, `/admin/payment-methods`)
- Show live Desktop/Mobile/Dark Customizer preview, Theme Presets, Order State Machine, and clarify COD, Bank Transfer, MyFatoorah, Fawry, Paymob, and Kashier (simulated in Demo Mode).

## 6. Analytics, Productivity & Commercial Administration (`/admin/analytics`, `/admin/import-export`, `/admin/system/*`)
- Show CSV analytics export, XLSX/CSV bulk import preview, RBAC roles, Audit Logs, Backups, System Health, Licensing, and `/install`.

## 7. Closing & Next Steps
- **Say (AR):** "كل اللي شفته جاهز وشغال فعليًا، ومعاه معالج تثبيت `/install` ودليل تشغيل ومركز مساعدة جوا لوحة التحكم. تحب نبدأ بتجهيز نسخة على الدومين بتاعك ولا نراجع متطلبات الدفع والشحن الأول؟"
- **Say (EN):** "Everything you saw is live and ready with `/install`, built-in `/admin/help`, and full backups. Would you like to deploy on your domain or review payment/shipping setup next?"
