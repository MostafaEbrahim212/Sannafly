# Standard Presentation (15 Minutes)

- **0:00–2:00 | Discovery & Opening:** Ask client needs, open `/demo/presentation` Slide 1.
- **2:00–5:00 | Storefront Experience (`/`, `/catalog`, `/cart`):** Filters, variations, wholesale pricing, coupons, shipping zones, simulated checkout.
- **5:00–8:00 | Catalog & Inventory (`/admin`, `/admin/products/create`, `/admin/categories`, `/admin/inventory`):** 5-step wizard, image gallery, stock ledger.
- **8:00–10:30 | Customizer & Themes (`/admin/customizer`, `/admin/customizer/themes`):** Live Desktop/Mobile/Dark preview and JSON presets.
- **10:30–13:00 | Orders, Payments & Analytics (`/admin/orders`, `/admin/analytics`):** Order State Machine, invoices/labels, CSV export.
- **13:00–15:00 | Security, Deployment & Close (`/admin/system/audit-logs`, `/admin/help`):** RBAC, backups, `/install`, and next steps.
